EFIR — ushlab olingan taktik radio tarmog'i

Operator qo'l qurilmasi hisoblagich blokini qayta ishlatgani sababli ko'plab uzatmalar bir xil himoya bilan yozilgan. Analitik xom base64 interceptlardan haqiqiy matnni qayta tiklashi lozim.

1-bosqich: bitta oynada ikki intercept

A1 — standart tarmoq ochilish preambulasi; A2 — shu oynadagi noma'lum trafik.

Yuklab olish: stage1/intercepts.txt
A1 ning hujjatlashtirilgan aniq matni (160 baytga bo'sh joy bilan to'ldirishdan oldin):
NET CONTROL TO ALL STATIONS THIS IS A ROUTINE RADIO CHECK ALL UNITS REPORT SIGNAL STRENGTH AND STANDBY FOR TRAFFIC MAINTAIN STRICT RADIO SILENCE UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE

2-bosqich: kunlik efir suhbati

24 ta base64 uzatma, hisoblagich qayta tiklangan, ochiq matn berilmagan — ulardan birida kerakli xabar bor.

Yuklab olish: stage2/intercepts.txt

3-bosqich: siljigan hisoblagichli kadrlar

Bir nechta kadr bir xil kalit/nonce, lekin turli hisoblagich siljishida; nishon uzatma har qanday bitta ma'lum kadrdan uzunroq.

Yuklab olish: stage3/intercepts.json
Hujjatlashtirilgan kadrlar
counter (blok)uzunlik (bayt)hujjatlashtirilgan ochiq matn (to'ldirishdan oldin)
648STATION SIX RELAYING WEATHER CONDITIONS ALONG THE
864SIX ACTUAL SENDS RESUPPLY STATUS FOR THE FORWARD ELEMENT NOW OK
1264RESUPPLY CONVOY DEPARTS AT ZERO FIVE HUNDRED HOURS ACKNOWLEDGE OK
Bayroq formati: CTF{…}. Har bir jamoa uchun alohida interceptlar; hech narsa umumiy emas.
